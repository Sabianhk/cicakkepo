Cicak Kepo

Cicak Kepo is an Indonesian animated science Shorts channel on YouTube: @CicakKepoChannel.

About the "Cicak Kepo" Google sign-in app

The "Cicak Kepo" Google OAuth app is a private production tool used only by the channel owner. After the owner signs in, it uploads the channel's own finished video files from the owner's computer into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and does not read, collect, or share data from anyone else.
